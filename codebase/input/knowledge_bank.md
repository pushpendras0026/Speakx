# SpeakX Knowledge Bank

## Company Vision & Mission
SpeakX is an AI-powered English speaking and communication learning app that helps Hindi and regional language speakers in India transform from "I understand English but can't speak" to confident English speakers through daily, guided speaking practice with our AI teacher "Sia".

## North Star Metric
**Daily Active Learners completing at least one speaking exercise** - This represents meaningful engagement that leads to learning outcomes and confidence building.

## Target Audience
- **Primary**: Working professionals (25-34) in Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities seeking career advancement
- **Secondary**: College students (18-24) preparing for placements and interviews
- **Tertiary**: Ambitious homemakers and mid-career professionals (35-44) seeking social confidence

## Product Features

### Core Learning Features
1. **Daily Lessons**: Structured 10-15 minute speaking exercises with AI feedback
2. **AI Tutor (Sia)**: Conversational practice with real-time pronunciation and grammar correction
3. **Role-Based Practice**: Job interviews, presentations, casual conversations, customer service scenarios
4. **Progress Reports**: Weekly summaries showing improvement in fluency, pronunciation, vocabulary

### Gamification Mechanics
1. **Coins**: Earned by completing exercises (10 coins/exercise), used to unlock bonus content
2. **Streaks**: Consecutive day practice tracking with streak protection feature
3. **Leaderboards**: Weekly regional and national rankings to drive competition
4. **Badges & Achievements**: Milestone rewards for consistency and improvement

### AI Companion Features
1. **Sia Chat**: 24/7 conversational practice partner
2. **Pronunciation Coach**: Real-time feedback on accent and clarity
3. **Grammar Guardian**: Contextual grammar corrections during practice
4. **Vocabulary Builder**: Personalized word recommendations based on learning level

## Ethical Communication Guidelines

### Allowed Tones
- Encouraging, Supportive, Celebratory, Motivational, Friendly, Professional, Empathetic

### Disallowed Tones
- Shaming, Guilt-tripping, Aggressive urgency, Manipulative, Condescending

### Communication Principles
1. Never shame users for not practicing
2. Focus on progress, not perfection
3. Celebrate small wins authentically
4. Respect user time and attention
5. Provide value in every notification
6. Use bilingual messaging (Hindi + English) for relatability

## User Journey Stages

### Trial Phase (Day 0-7)
- **Goal**: Demonstrate value, build habit, drive conversion
- **Focus Features**: First lesson completion, AI tutor introduction, streak initiation
- **Key Moments**: Day 1 (activation), Day 3 (habit seed), Day 5 (value realization), Day 7 (conversion)

### Paid Phase (Day 8-30)
- **Goal**: Deepen engagement, expand feature usage, build mastery
- **Focus Features**: Advanced lessons, leaderboard participation, role-based practice
- **Key Moments**: Day 14 (feature expansion), Day 21 (habit lock), Day 30 (renewal prep)

### Retention Phase (Day 31+)
- **Goal**: Maintain habit, drive referrals, build loyalty
- **Focus Features**: Progress milestones, community features, advanced content
- **Key Moments**: Monthly progress review, streak milestones, referral prompts

### Churned/Inactive Phase
- **Goal**: Win-back with value reminder, not pressure
- **Focus Features**: Progress preservation, easy re-entry, special offers
- **Key Moments**: Day 3 inactive (soft nudge), Day 7 inactive (value reminder), Day 14+ (win-back offer)
